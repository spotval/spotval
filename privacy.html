Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026 · Last updated: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SpotVal ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website at spotval.vercel.app (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you use SpotVal to score a listing, you enter information about that listing — such as the title, asking price, condition, and description. This text is sent to our AI scoring service to generate a deal score and recommendation. We do not store this information on our servers after the request completes.

Automatically collected information

We automatically collect limited information when you visit the Service:

  • IP address. Used to enforce the daily scan limit (5 scans per IP per 24 hours). IP addresses are held in temporary server memory only and are not written to a persistent database.
  • Behavioral analytics. We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site through anonymous session recordings, heatmaps, and aggregate usage metrics. See "Third-Party Services" below.
  • Browser-side storage. We use your browser's local storage to remember your scan history (last 5 scans) and your remaining daily scan count. This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Generate deal scores and recommendations for the listings you submit.
  • Enforce the daily scan limit so the service stays available for all users.
  • Understand which features visitors use, identify usability issues, and improve the Service.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services to operate the Service. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how it handles your information.

Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity provides session recording, heatmap, and analytics features. Clarity may collect data about your device, browser, IP address, mouse movements, clicks, scrolls, and the pages you visit on our site. This data is processed by Microsoft and is subject to Microsoft's Privacy Statement. You can opt out of Clarity tracking by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser or by using a content-blocking extension such as uBlock Origin.

GitHub Models (AI scoring)

The deal-scoring functionality is powered by an AI model hosted on GitHub Models. When you submit a listing, the text you provide is sent to GitHub Models for analysis. Use of this service is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not include any personal identifiers in the requests we send.

Vercel

Our website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard server-log information (IP address, user agent, requested URL, response status) for security and performance monitoring. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

Formsubmit (contact form)

Our contact form is processed by Formsubmit, which forwards submissions to our team's email inbox. Information you submit through the contact form (name, email address, subject, message) is handled per Formsubmit's privacy practices.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SpotVal does not set any first-party cookies for tracking or advertising. Microsoft Clarity may set cookies as part of its analytics functionality. We use browser local storage (a similar technology) to save your scan history and daily limit locally — this data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

5. Data Retention

  • Listing text you submit: Not stored after analysis. Held only in memory for the duration of the request.
  • IP addresses for rate limiting: Held in temporary server memory and reset roughly every 24 hours.
  • Clarity analytics: Retained by Microsoft per Clarity's default retention policy (currently 13 months).
  • Browser local storage: Persists on your device until you clear it or your browser's site data is removed.
  • Contact-form submissions: Retained in our email inbox until manually deleted.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access. Request information about what data we hold about you.
  • Deletion. Request deletion of any data we hold. Because we do not store listing text and do not maintain user accounts, the only data tied to you is your IP-based rate-limit count (which automatically resets) and any Clarity session data (which you can opt out of as described above).
  • Opt-out of analytics. Block Microsoft Clarity using a content-blocking browser extension or by enabling Do Not Track.
  • Clear local storage. Clear your scan history at any time by clicking "Clear history" in the Recent Scans section, or by clearing your browser's site data for spotval.vercel.app.

To exercise any of these rights or to ask a question about your data, contact us through our contact form or by email at spotvalapp@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

SpotVal is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States and by our third-party service providers, which may have different data-protection laws than your jurisdiction.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the Service evolves — for example, when we add features such as user accounts, payments, or affiliate integrations. The "Effective date" at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle your data, reach us through our contact form or by email at spotvalapp@gmail.com.

Note. This policy reflects our current practices and is updated as SpotVal evolves. For specific legal questions about how this policy applies to your situation, consult a qualified attorney.
